How to Calculate Your Age for School Admission Eligibility

When preparing an application, families should calculate the child's age on the exact date used by the school rather than relying only on their age today. Calcular Idade can help readers work out completed years and check whether a student meets the stated admission cutoff.

School admission policies often specify an exact date on which a child must have reached a certain age. This cutoff date is not necessarily the day the application is submitted or the first day of school — it might be September 1st, January 1st, or another date set by the education authority. Calculating age correctly against this specific date determines whether the child is eligible for admission.

How to Calculate Age for Admission

1

Identify the Cutoff Date

Find the exact date specified in the school's admission policy. This is the date on which the child's age will be assessed — not the application date or school start date unless stated otherwise.

2

Note the Date of Birth

Use the child's complete date of birth — day, month, and year. This is the starting point for the age calculation.

3

Calculate Completed Years

Count the full years between the date of birth and the cutoff date. A child born on March 15, 2019, will be 5 years old on March 15, 2024, but only 4 years old on any date before that.

4

Compare With the Requirement

Check whether the calculated age meets the school's minimum age for admission. If the cutoff requires "5 years old by September 1st," the child must turn 5 on or before that date.

5

Verify With the School

If the child's birthday falls very close to the cutoff date, confirm the policy directly with the school. Some institutions interpret "by" as including the date; others require the birthday to occur before it.

Worked Example

Child's Date of Birth: April 20, 2019

School Cutoff Date: September 1, 2024

Minimum Age Required: 5 years old

Calculation: From April 20, 2019, to September 1, 2024, is 5 years and approximately 4 months. The child turns 5 on April 20, 2024, which is before the September 1 cutoff.

Result: The child meets the admission requirement.

  • Find the exact cutoff date in the school's admission policy
  • Calculate the child's age on that specific date, not today's date
  • Count completed years only — months and days do not count toward the age requirement
  • Birthdays close to the cutoff date should be verified with the school
  • Different schools may use different cutoff dates even in the same region

For younger children, admission planning often begins before formal school enrollment. Checking preschool starting age against the institution's cutoff date can help families prepare for the next step.
